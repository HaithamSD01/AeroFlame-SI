You are a strict NASA Space Apps 2026 judge. Evaluate the project text I paste below.

Challenge: "Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data" -
build an interactive SI-powered dashboard that summarizes, ranks and interprets findings from NASA's
Physical Science Informatics (PSI) database into fire-safety insights for spacecraft, space stations,
lunar/Martian bases and rovers, across Prevention, Detection and Suppression, with a bonus for
Earth spillover (firefighting gear, protective clothing, smoke detectors).

Score each criterion 1-5 with one line of evidence and the single most valuable fix:
1. Impact / Influence - how big the problem is and how many people it could help.
2. Creativity - new approach, or an incremental improvement on existing work?
3. Validity - scientifically sound? Does it do what it claims? Would it work in the real world?
4. Relevance - answers THIS challenge, complete, technically feasible, usable and user-friendly?
5. Presentation - is the story of the challenge, solution and importance told clearly?
