AeroFlame SI

Fire safety for spacecraft, lunar and Mars habitats, where smoke does not rise. A small sensor node reads flame, smoke and heat, and this platform turns them into one risk level, tells dust from fire, and shows the NASA microgravity combustion findings behind each alert.

Built by team Atlas Cosmos (Casablanca) for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Flame in Freefall". Code: github.com/HaithamSD01/AeroFlame-SI.
